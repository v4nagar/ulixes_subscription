Sell products and services with recurring payments, free trials, sign-up fees, renewals, and switching in WooCommerce.

== Description ==

Ulixes Subscriptions is a complete recurring billing and subscription management solution for WooCommerce.

= Features =
* Simple and Variable Subscription product types
* Flexible billing schedules (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, every 1st to 6th interval)
* Free trials & Sign-up fees
* Automatic and manual renewal payments via Action Scheduler
* Built-in Failed Recurring Payment Retry engine
* Upgrades, Downgrades & Crossgrades with recurring price and sign-up fee proration
* Synchronised renewal dates with proration and grace periods
* Early renewal, Resubscribe, Customer self-service Suspend / Reactivate / Cancel, and Auto-Renew toggle
* Subscription coupons (Sign-up fee & Recurring discounts, plus limited-payment coupons)
* 14 transactional subscription emails (HTML & Plain Text)
* Full High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS), Cart/Checkout Blocks, and WP REST API (`/wc/v3/subscriptions`) support

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `ulixes-subscriptions` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Configure your subscription settings under **WooCommerce > Settings > Subscriptions**.
